## Detailed Phase Breakdown

The encounter progresses through three distinct, increasingly aggressive phases. Transition thresholds occur at 70% and 35% health pools.

```
[Phase 1: 100% - 70%] ──> [Phase 2: 70% - 35%] ──> [Phase 3: 35% - 0%]
  Methodical Swings        Ceiling Ambushes         Enraged Frenzy
  Arena Navigation         Sanity Hazards           Unblockable Sweeps
```

### Phase 1: The Calculated Scalpel (100% – 70% HP)

During the opening phase, the entity moves with deceptive grace. It primarily relies on wide horizontal swings using elongated surgical appendages.

| Attack Name | Visual / Audio Cue | Dodge Direction | Counterattack Window |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Surgical Cleave** | Raises right blade overhead; metallic hiss | Dodge backward twice | 1.5 seconds (Flank left) |
| **Blink Lunge** | Disappears into shadows; floor sparkles | Side-roll at sound of footstep | 2.0 seconds (Strike rear node) |
| **Neurotoxin Mist** | Chest cavity vents purple vapors | Back away immediately | Ranged weapon fire only |

Maintain a mid-distance perimeter around the central operating table. When the boss performs the *Blink Lunge*, roll immediately to the right and punish the prolonged recovery frames with your primary firearm.

---

### Phase 2: Aerial Ambush and Arena Hazards (70% – 35% HP)

Once reduced to 70% health, the creature screeches and leaps onto the overhead surgical lighting tracks. The arena lights will flicker, significantly reducing visibility.

1. **Track Ceiling Movement:** Watch for dislodged dust particles and shadows moving across the ceiling tiles.
2. **Evade the Plunge:** When the boss dives downward, it creates a shockwave covering a 5-meter radius. Sprint outward, then jump or dodge clear of the ground tremor.
3. **Destroy Ambient Nodes:** While airborne, the boss spawns pulsating flesh cysts on the walls that drain your sanity. Take these down quickly with single pistol rounds.

| Phase 2 Hazard | Threat Level | Removal Method | Consequence If Ignored |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Spore Cysts** | Moderate | 1x Precision Pistol Shot | Continuous screen distortion & aim sway |
| **Shattered Glass** | Low | Avoid walking over tiles | Stagger and noise alerts to the boss |
| **Overhead Slam** | Fatal | Timed lateral sprint + roll | Instant kill or severe critical wound |

---

### Phase 3: Desperation Frenzy (35% – 0% HP)

Entering the final stretch of the **Anomalith boss fight**, the monster discards all defensive posture. It enters a permanent frenzy state, chaining rapid multi-hit combos while bleeding corrosive fluid across the center ring.

| Boss Action | Reaction Strategy | Risk Factor |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Quad-Combo Flurry** | Backpedal while dodge-rolling on hits 2 and 4 | Extreme (Guaranteed knockdown) |
| **Acid Discharge** | Do not enter the center ring; fight strictly along perimeter walls | High (Rapid armor erosion) |
| **Desperation Grab** | Look for glowing red chest core; fire a high-stagger shot directly into it | Fatal (One-hit execution) |

During this phase, do not attempt extended melee strings or slow reloads. Rely on burst damage, bait out the three-strike combo, and lay into the exposed chest core to end the fight decisively.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the easiest build for the Anomalith boss fight?
Community testing shows that a mobility-focused build paired with a high-burst shotgun and sanity-stabilizing charms makes the **Anomalith boss fight** much more manageable, allowing you to quickly punish short recovery windows.

### How do I stop the sanity meter from draining in the Operating Room?
Equipping the Neuro-Anchor trinket before the battle drastically slows sanity depletion. Additionally, promptly shooting down the glowing cysts spawned during Phase 2 prevents large spikes in mental degradation.

### Can you parry the boss's attacks in the Anomalith boss fight?
Only standard single-blade slashes in Phase 1 can be parried with precise timing. Multi-hit flurries, lunges, and Phase 3 frenzy sweeps are completely unblockable and must be dodged or out-spaced.
