## What Is Anomalith? Game Overview and Key Talent

Developed and published by FuRyu Corporation, *Anomalith* is an intense third-person survival horror shooter that transports players to a distorted, supernatural vision of modern Japan. You step into the shoes of investigator Reona Minazuki as she explores dangerous "anomaly zones," battles grotesque entities, and searches for her missing companion.

The game is penned by celebrated scenario writer Romeo Tanaka (*Humanity Has Declined*, *Rewrite*), ensuring a multi-layered, psychologically gripping narrative. Elevating the atmosphere even further is an evocative soundtrack composed by Yuka Kitamura, legendary for her iconic and haunting musical contributions to acclaimed atmospheric action RPGs.

## Core Gameplay Mechanics & Innovations

*Anomalith* stands out within the modern survival horror space by fusing tight third-person gunplay with surreal liminal-space exploration and dynamic supernatural progression.

### The Demi-Humanization Progression System

Rather than relying purely on standard firearm upgrades, *Anomalith* features the **Demi-Humanization System**. By defeating anomaly entities and absorbing their residual powers, Reona unlocks active supernatural attacks and passive survival traits. This mechanic lets players build tailored loadouts suited to aggressive close-range encounters or tactical stealth builds.

### Key Feature Comparison

| Feature | Mechanic Details | Player Impact |
