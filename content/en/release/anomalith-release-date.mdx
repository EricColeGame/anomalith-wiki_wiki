## Story Setting, Characters, and Creative All-Star Team

The setting of *ANOMALITH* thrusts players into an alternate modern Japan during the year 20██. Bizarre occurrences known as "Anomaly Zones" materialize without warning across urban landscapes. The protagonist, 17-year-old Reona Minazuki, is shopping at a commercial mall with her best friend Arisa Kato when a sudden "Anomalization" tears the facility apart, leaving Arisa missing.

Reona enlists as a specialized "Diver" in the government-backed Anomaly Zone Countermeasures Office to track down her companion. During her catastrophic first dive, an unknown supernatural power awakens within her, turning her into the sole survivor capable of navigating these reality-bending environments.

## Core Gameplay Loop: Dive, Retrieve, and Return

At its core, *ANOMALITH* blends tactical third-person shooter gunplay with extraction survival mechanics and action-RPG progression. Survival requires striking a delicate balance between aggressive combat and conservative supply runs. Knowing how these systems work ahead of the **ANOMALITH release date** gives you a major strategic edge.

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│               THE ANOMALITH GAMEPLAY LOOP              │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│  1. PREPARE     → Loadout Weapons, Ammo, & Outfits     │
│  2. DIVE        → Enter Reality-Bending Anomaly Zones  │
│  3. SURVIVE     → Engage Hostile Anomalies via TPS/Skills│

│  5. RETURN      → Extract Safely & Craft Upgrades      │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### Strategic Combat & Anomalization Skills

Standard firearms only tell half the story. While navigating shifting environments inspired by liminal spaces and 1980s retro aesthetics, you will face hostile entities known as Anomalies. 

- **Tactical Weapon Switching:** Different Anomalies exhibit specific vulnerabilities; swapping between close-range shotguns, rapid-fire sidearms, and precision rifles is essential.
- **Anomalization Skills:** Reona wields both offensive and defensive psychic abilities that alter the flow of battle, stun high-threat targets, or shield her from lethal spatial anomalies.
- **Visual Customization (Transmog):** Upgrades alter weapon and armor statistics, but player appearance can be modified independently, letting you customize outfits without taking stat penalties.
