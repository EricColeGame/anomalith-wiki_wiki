## What Is ANOMALITH? Story and Premise

Set in an alternate Japan during the year 20██, surreal Anomaly Zones begin manifesting across the country without prior warning. These supernatural breaches alter ordinary environments into distorted liminal nightmares filled with hostile, grotesque creatures dubbed "Anomalies."

The narrative follows 17-year-old high schooler Reona Minazuki. During a routine shopping trip at a local mall, an Anomalization event traps Reona and separates her from her best friend, Arisa Kato. Refusing to accept her friend's disappearance, Reona signs up as an official "Diver" for the government-sponsored Anomaly Zone Countermeasures Office.

| Key Narrative Element | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Protagonist** | Reona Minazuki (Voiced by Hitomi Ueda), a resilient 17-year-old Diver seeking her lost friend. |
| **Core Mystery** | The sudden manifestation of Anomaly Zones and the awakening of Reona's supernatural powers. |
| **Primary Antagonists** | Bizarre biological and surreal entities known collectively as Anomalies. |
| **Setting Aesthetic** | Showa-era nostalgic architecture combined with unsettling, dreamlike liminal spaces. |
| **Supporting Cast** | Arisa Kato, Office Chief Kyoka Sudo, Lead Researcher Imari Fushimi, and fellow Diver Kaede Mori. |

On her very first expedition, Reona awakens dormant combat abilities after witnessing other seasoned Divers succumb to the zone's terrors. Armed with specialized gear and uncanny combat techniques, she must descend into ever-shifting zones to uncover the truth behind her powers and save Arisa.

## Core Gameplay Mechanics: The "Dive, Retrieve, Return" Loop

At its heart, the game functions as a survival action RPG and third-person shooter centered on a disciplined gameplay loop: **Dive, Retrieve, and Return**. Survival demands careful preparation before entering each zone and ruthless resource management while fighting through it.

```
[Prepare Loadout] -> [Dive into Anomaly Zone] -> [Battle Anomalies & Scavenge] -> [Extract Safely] -> [Upgrade Gear & Workshop]
```

### 1. Tactical Third-Person Combat

Engaging Anomalies is never purely run-and-gun. Players must balance firearm usage with specialized offensive and defensive Anomalization Skills. Managing ammunition reserves, choosing between close-range shotguns or precision rifles, and tracking spatial awareness in claustrophobic corridors dictate whether you survive an expedition.

### 2. Deep Gear Crafting and Aesthetics

Items, anomaly fragments, and materials extracted from runs are brought back to the Workshop. Imari Fushimi's research lab allows you to craft new firearms, upgrade base stats, and engineer specialized survival gadgets. Crucially, the cosmetic system lets you change Reona's visual gear and outfits independently of equipment stats, enabling complete cosmetic customization without sacrificing defensive armor values.

| Combat & Survival System | Strategic Purpose | Player Decision Factor |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Ammunition Conservation** | Resources inside zones are scarce. | Firearm precision vs. melee/skill usage. |
| **Anomalization Skills** | Deployable offensive bursts and defensive barriers. | Cooldown timing during overwhelming swarms. |
| **Extraction Mechanics** | Reach designated extraction zones to secure loot. | Risking deeper exploration vs. securing current haul. |
| **Transmog System** | Aesthetic freedom without compromising gear stats. | Purely cosmetic tailoring for your Diver. |
