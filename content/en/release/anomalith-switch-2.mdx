## Story Overview: Surviving the Anomaly Zones

Set in a near-future Japan during the year 20██, reality begins to unravel as catastrophic "Anomaly Zones" manifest without warning across urban landscapes. The government forms the Anomaly Zone Countermeasures Office to scout, contain, and retrieve resources from these reality-distorting pockets.

You step into the combat boots of **Reona Minazuki**, a 17-year-old girl who volunteers to become an Anomaly Diver after her closest friend vanishes into one of the Zones. During her disastrous maiden Dive, Reona's entire squad is wiped out by bizarre eldritch entities known simply as "Anomalies." 

As the lone survivor, a hidden paranormal mutation awakens within Reona, granting her supernatural capabilities. Armed with tactical firearms and evolving powers, she must descend deeper into the abyss to uncover the truth behind her friend's disappearance, her mutating body, and the origin of the anomalies.

### Anomaly Zone Exploration Mechanics

The environments within the Zones defy normal physical laws, turning familiar urban spaces into disorienting spatial puzzles:

* **Inverted Gravity Complexes:** Navigate residential apartment buildings where floors, walls, and ceilings swap places dynamically.
* **Writhing Hallways:** Narrow indoor corridors that twist, stretch, and alter sightlines mid-firefight.
* **Multiplying Shopping Districts:** Endless commercial streets that replicate infinitely, requiring players to track markers and avoid endless loops.

## Beginner Strategy Guide: How to Prepare for Dives

Mastering the high-stress environment of the Zones requires disciplined habits. Here are foundational rules every Diver should follow when diving into the action on **ANOMALITH Switch 2**:

1. **Prioritize Route Planning Over Combat:** You do not earn bonuses simply for clearing every standard mob. If an encounter burns through too much ammunition, bypass it using lateral corridors.
2. **Balance Inventory Slots:** Always leave at least four open inventory slots when embarking. Filling your bag with basic supplies leaves no room for rare Artifacts found near extraction points.
3. **Master Skill Rotations:** Reona’s 12 Anomalization skills are not panic buttons—they are offensive multipliers. Pair crowd-control abilities with close-range shotgun blasts for rapid clears.
4. **Study the Dive Reports:** Every defeated creature logs vital ecological data in your base archive. Reviewing elemental vulnerabilities drastically cuts down time-to-kill ratios.

As you dive into **ANOMALITH Switch 2**, refining these fundamentals early on will prevent costly gear losses during deep anomaly excursions.
