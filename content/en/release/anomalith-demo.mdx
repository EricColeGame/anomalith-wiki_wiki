## Core Gameplay Systems and Progression Loop

At its heart, the game bridges psychological survival horror with responsive third-person shooter mechanics. Surviving an expedition requires balancing your offensive firearm loadout with active supernatural skills awakened inside the zone.

```
[ Enter Anomaly Zone ] ➔ [ Scavenge Materials & Objects ] ➔ [ Defeat Anomalies ] ➔ [ Secure Extraction ] ➔ [ Craft Gear & Upgrade Skills ]
```

## Tips for Surviving Your First Dive

Taking your first steps into an Anomaly Zone can be disorienting. Keep these practical tips in mind while navigating the **anomalith demo**:

1. **Prioritize Sound Cues:** Anomalies often produce distinct audio distortions before appearing visual in liminal corridors. Use headphones with directional audio enabled.
2. **Conserve Ammo on Trash Mobs:** Do not waste high-tier shotgun shells or rifle cartridges on standard wandering entities; rely on sidearm precision shots to weak points.
3. **Monitor Sanity/Sedative Levels:** Prolonged exposure to surreal geometry drains mental focus. Keep sedatives hotkeyed for sudden difficulty spikes.
4. **Bank Upgrades Early:** When exploring in the **anomalith demo**, return to extraction points as soon as key crafting parts are secured rather than risking a total wipe for marginal loot.
