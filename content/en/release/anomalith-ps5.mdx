## What Is ANOMALITH? Story and Premise

Set in an alternate, near-future Japan during the 20XX era, *ANOMALITH* thrusts players into a reality fractured by catastrophic occurrences known as Anomaly Zones. These mysterious territories defy standard physics, giving rise to reality-bending spaces like inverted gravity complexes, endless repeating shopping districts, and writhing corridors populated by nightmarish entities called Anomalies.

Players assume the role of **Reona Minazuki**, a 17-year-old student who enlists as a specialized "Diver" for the government-backed Anomaly Zone Countermeasures Office. Driven by the search for her missing friend, Reona undertakes dangerous expeditions into these corrupted realms. During her catastrophic first dive, when all other operatives perish, she discovers a latent mutation within herself—awakening powerful "Anomalization Skills" that become crucial for survival.

## Core Gameplay Mechanics: The "Dive, Retrieve, Return" Loop

At its heart, **ANOMALITH PS5** delivers an extraction-inspired single-player survival TPS (Third-Person Shooter) framework. The gameplay centers on three interconnected phases: diving into dangerous spaces, gathering high-value supplies, and safely extracting back to base.

```
[ Preparation & Base Crafting ] ---> [ Dive into Anomaly Zone ]
               ^                                   |
               |                                   v
       [ Extraction to Base ] <--- [ Combat, Loot & Extraction ]
```

### Anomaly Zone Exploration and Combat

Every dive challenges your spatial awareness and resource discipline. Inside Anomaly Zones, traditional environmental rules no longer apply. You must balance accurate gunplay against resource scarcity, knowing when to fight and when to disengage.

| Gameplay Feature | Mechanic Description | Tactical Value |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Anomalous Navigation** | Non-Euclidean spaces, shifting layouts, inverted gravity | Demands constant environmental scanning |
| **Resource Management** | Restricted ammo pool and limited backpack capacity | Forces calculated combat engagements |
| **Anomalization Skills** | 12 unique awakened powers usable by Reona | Provides crowd control, evasion, and burst damage |
| **Field Extraction** | Reaching designated return points while under threat | High risk, high reward loot retention |

### Base Camp Progression and Customization

Your operations outside the zones take place at Base Camp. Here, you prepare for future dives by spending recovered materials and researching hostile entities.

| Base Feature | Functionality | Player Benefit |

## Combat Strategy and Beginner Tips for Anomaly Zones

Succeeding in the twisted corridors of **ANOMALITH PS5** requires deliberate preparation. Player reports and initial gameplay reveals highlight several key strategies for staying alive:

1. **Prioritize Ammo Conservation:** Firearm rounds are scarce. Combine primary weapon fire with Reona's 12 Anomalization skills to finish off weakened anomalies without emptying magazines.
2. **Utilize Transmog for Strategic Freedom:** Gear provides mechanical stat benefits across nine individual slots. Because appearance can be overridden freely, you never have to sacrifice optimal stats for style.
3. **Study Ecology Reports:** Defeating new threats adds entries to your Base Dive Reports. Reviewing these notes reveals elemental or behavioral weaknesses that simplify future encounters.
4. **Plan Safe Extractions:** When inventory space fills up, do not push recklessly into deeper layers. Securing your current artifacts is vital for steady weapon upgrades.

**ANOMALITH PS5** officially launches on **October 28, 2026, at 03:00 PM UTC**. Players who purchase the Digital Deluxe Edition receive 48-hour early access, allowing them to start playing on October 26, 2026.

### Does ANOMALITH require an internet connection to play?
No. **ANOMALITH PS5** is built as a complete single-player experience with full offline play support enabled on your primary console.

### What are the pre-order bonuses for ANOMALITH?
Pre-ordering either the Standard or Digital Deluxe edition grants access to the "Blue Powered Suit" costume alongside the *Lost Property Control Organization* crossover bundle, which includes an exclusive side mission, outfit, and the "T-6407J" drone skin.

### Can you change your outfit without losing armor stats?
Yes. The game features a 9-slot gear system where clothing items dictate your combat stats, but outward cosmetics can be customized independently without altering your build's performance.
