## Detailed Profiles: Divers, Operatives, and Civilians

The psychological depth of the **Anomalith characters** drives both the central campaign and optional side content. As revealed on the [ANOMALITH Official Website](https://www.cs.furyu.jp/anomalith/en/), players can unlock extensive Dive Reports and relationship data by finishing specialized side missions.

### Reona Minazuki (File #00000-01)
* **Voice Actor:** Hitomi Ueda
* **Age:** 17
* **Affiliation:** Anomaly Zone Countermeasures Office

Reona Minazuki serves as the playable protagonist of the game. After running away from a broken home environment, she survived on the streets where she met Arisa Kato. When an unexpected anomaly consumed the shopping mall they were visiting, the two became separated. Reona survived through mysterious means and subsequently enlisted as a government Diver.

During her first official Dive, when all other squad members perished, Reona awakened latent supernatural powers. Driven entirely by the goal of recovering Arisa, she plunges deeper into the distorted urban spaces where gravity inverts and hallways warp endlessly.

### Arisa Kato (File #00000-02)
* **Voice Actor:** Natsumi Haruse
* **Affiliation:** Koin Girls' Academy

Arisa Kato is a high-society student attending the elite Koin Girls' Academy. Despite having an upbringing completely opposite to Reona's harsh street life, the two formed an unbreakable bond. Arisa went missing immediately after the shopping mall anomalized, serving as the emotional catalyst for Reona's perilous descent into the Zones.

### Kyoka Sudo (File #00000-04)
* **Voice Actor:** Keiko Watanabe
* **Affiliation:** Anomaly Zone Countermeasures Office

The stoic founder and commanding chief of the Anomaly Zone Countermeasures Office. Rational to an extreme degree, Kyoka is a survivor who spent extended periods trapped within an Anomaly Zone before finding an extraction point. She climbed the organizational ladder from a disposable frontline Diver all the way to agency chief, commanding absolute respect through ironclad composure.

### Supporting Base Personnel and Mysterious Entities

The Countermeasures Office functions through several key personnel who directly aid your expeditions:

* **Yuiga Koda (File #00000-03):** Tactical operator voiced by Riho Sugiyama. Formerly a frontline Diver, she suffered disabling injuries during her initial Dive and now coordinates missions from the safety of base command.
* **Imari Fushimi (File #00000-05):** Head of Research and Development, voiced by Shion Wakayama. Imari converts hazardous anomalous materials into tactical weapons and gear. Despite her calm demeanor, she harbors a reckless passion for anomaly research.
* **May Hibiya (File #00000-06):** Head of Security, voiced by Hikari Kubota. May ensures that anomalous contaminations and external threats do not compromise headquarters.
* **Kaede Mori (File #00000-07):** Voiced by Natsumi Fujiwara, Kaede is a rogue wanderer encountered within the shifting depths of the Zone whose motives remain obscured.
* **The Girl (File #00000-08):** A young, nameless child found in the anomalized mall who fell completely silent after witnessing her parents eliminated by Anomalies.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Who is the main character in Anomalith?
The main protagonist is 17-year-old Reona Minazuki, voiced by Hitomi Ueda. She joins the government-backed Anomaly Zone Countermeasures Office as a Diver to search for her missing best friend, Arisa Kato.

### How many Anomalith characters are officially confirmed?
There are eight core characters officially documented in the classified agency records: Reona Minazuki, Arisa Kato, Yuiga Koda, Kyoka Sudo, Imari Fushimi, May Hibiya, Kaede Mori, and the enigmatic traumatized girl.

### How do interactions with other characters affect gameplay?
Interacting with support personnel at the base allows you to craft ammunition, upgrade specialized firearms with attachments, analyze creature ecology via Dive Reports, and alter Reona's combat stats using a nine-piece gear customization system.

### What platforms is Anomalith releasing on?
*ANOMALITH* is scheduled to launch worldwide on PlayStation 5, Nintendo Switch 2, and Steam (PC).
