### Who is the anomalith girl listed as File #00000-08?
The nameless girl is a young survivor who temporarily joined Reona Minazuki and Arisa Kato when a shopping mall anomalized. After witnessing the death of her parents at the hands of Anomalies, she fell into traumatic mutism.

### Does the anomalith girl possess combat powers?
Official Countermeasures Office files list her without combat capabilities or agency affiliation. However, player speculation suggests her connection to the mall incident could reveal crucial narrative details regarding how Anomaly Zones manifest.

### What platforms is ANOMALITH releasing on?
*ANOMALITH* is scheduled to launch worldwide on PlayStation 5, Nintendo Switch 2, and PC via Steam, featuring single-player anomaly exploration with music by composer Yuka Kitamura.
