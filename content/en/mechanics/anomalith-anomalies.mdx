## Understanding Anomaly Zones: Reality-Bending Environments

Anomaly Zones are surreal pocket dimensions that suddenly materialized across modern Japan. Unlike standard battlefields, these sectors completely discard the known laws of physics. Navigating them requires spatial awareness and constant adaptation.

When venturing into a Dive, you will encounter diverse environmental mutations that directly alter your tactical options.

| Zone Environment Type | Core Spatial Phenomenon | Tactical Combat Impact | Recommended Preparation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Inverted Apartment Complexes** | Reversed and shifting gravity planes | Flanking enemies from ceilings; fall hazards | Mid-range rifles, high-mobility gear |
| **Writhing Hallways** | Shifting walls and undulating corridors | Tight choke points, sudden ambush angles | Shotguns, close-quarters melee skills |
| **Multiplying Shopping Streets** | Endlessly duplicating storefronts and loops | Disorientation, respawning flanking paths | Navigation markers, high-capacity magazines |
| **Abyssal Convergence Hubs** | Compressed space and visual distortions | Reduced visibility, erratic projectile trajectories | Artifacts boosting perception, thermal attachments |

Surviving these zones requires mastering the core loop: **Dive, Retrieve, and Return**. Every step deeper into an Anomaly Zone increases both the potential value of retrieved Artifacts and the hostility of surrounding threats.

## Threat Breakdown: Types of Anomalith Anomalies

The primary threats inside the Zones are the hostile entities known collectively as Anomalies. These bizarre organisms vary wildly in form, aggression patterns, and combat behaviors. Dealing with **Anomalith anomalies** requires matching your weapon attachments and elemental loadouts to their unique biological structures.

Defeating these creatures unlocks detailed entries in your **Dive Reports**, revealing critical lore and biological data regarding their origins.

### Combat Tactics Against Anomalous Entities

When engaging **Anomalith anomalies** during high-tier extraction runs:
- **Prioritize Ammo Conservation**: Standard rounds are strictly limited; do not waste high-tier ammunition on roaming fodder.
- **Target Biological Weak Points**: Most anomalous creatures feature distinct glowing nodes or morphing tissue that take amplified critical damage.
- **Leverage Environmental Hazard**: Lure fast-moving entities into gravity distortions or shifting corridors to bottle them up.

## 5 Essential Survival Rules for Zone Divers

To ensure consistent extractions while battling **Anomalith anomalies**, keep these community-tested rules in mind:

1. **Never Overstay Your Route**: Zones destabilize over time. Collect high-value Artifacts and prioritize heading toward extraction before supply lines collapse.
2. **Read the Dive Reports**: Every defeated creature adds biological notes to your database. Reviewing these reports reveals exact elemental weaknesses and behavioral tells.
3. **Balance Inventory Slots**: Do not fill your pack exclusively with ammo or crafting scrap. Always leave space for rare Artifacts that permanently upgrade base facilities.
4. **Rotate Anomalization Skills**: Holding onto your mutant powers causes tactical bottlenecks. Use skills frequently to conserve scarce standard ammunition.
5. **Upgrade Base Infrastructure**: Invest salvaged components into base development sets to unlock higher-tier firearm attachments and expanded medical kits.
