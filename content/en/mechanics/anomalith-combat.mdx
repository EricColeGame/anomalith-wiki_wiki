## Core Fundamentals of Anomalith Combat

At its foundation, combat in *Anomalith* blends classic third-person tactical shooting (TPS) with an escalating biological risk-reward mechanic known as the "Instability Engine." Unlike traditional shooters where health and stamina are your primary concerns, every offensive and defensive action alters your character's physiological state. 

Engaging in extended firefights or absorbing anomaly-laced attacks increases your Mutation Gauge. When managed properly, mutations grant superhuman speed, hardened bone armor, and devastating melee tentacles. However, if your gauge overflows, your character enters a state of cellular collapse, severely draining health and compromising weapon handling.

### Weapon Modding and Synergy

Every ballistic weapon features two mod slots: an **Optic/Handling Socket** and a **Biological Catalyst Port**. Installing biological mods allows your bullets to inflict specific status conditions like *Cellular Rot*, which weakens enemy kinetic defenses, or *Resonance Echo*, causing targets to detonate upon death.

* Always prioritize magazine capacity mods over raw damage for fast-firing weapons, as sustained fire prevents anomaly shield regeneration.
* Pair high-damage single-shot weapons with *Catalyst Leech* to regenerate small increments of stamina on critical hits.
* Keep at least one weapon reserved for un-mutated kinetic ammo to deal with baseline mechanical and human hostiles.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What makes Anomalith combat different from other survival shooters?
Unlike typical shooters that focus strictly on ammunition conservation and cover mechanics, **Anomalith combat** incorporates an escalating mutation gauge. Players must intentionally balance firearm gunplay with biological body modifications, shifting between human accuracy and mutated power sets while avoiding full biological collapse.

### How do I prevent cellular collapse during intense fights?
Cellular collapse occurs when your Instability Meter reaches 100%. To prevent this, equip *Pure Catalyst Injectors* or execute an *Anomalous Purge* when your gauge sits between 75% and 95%. This releases excess energy in an explosive attack while resetting your baseline human stats.

### Can I complete the game using only traditional firearms?
While ballistic firearms deal reliable damage against baseline threats, higher-tier anomalies feature dynamic armor plating that is highly resistant to standard bullets. Efficient **Anomalith combat** requires you to weave mutation abilities into your rotation to strip shields, stagger heavy targets, and conserve scarce ammo.

### Which mutation stage is best for beginner players?
Stage 1 (*Tendon Warp*) is widely regarded by community reports as the safest mutation tier for newcomers. It unlocks valuable movement speed, faster reloads, and a versatile grapple attack without imposing the severe mobility or health drain penalties found in deeper stages.
