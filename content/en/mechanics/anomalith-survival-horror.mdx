## Tactical Combat Tips for Surviving Encounters

To maintain your edge against the horrors lurking in the dark, implement these practical rules during exploration and combat:

- **Conserve Ballistic Ammo for Weak Points:** Standard ammunition barely scratches advanced biomorphic horrors unless targeted at glowing cellular nodes. Use melee strikes or environmental traps to stagger targets first.
- **Listen for Acoustic Shifts:** The sound design heavily telegraphs incoming spatial anomalies. A subtle rise in static or localized bass hums indicates an anomaly is about to materialize nearby.
- **Cycle Stabilization Wisely:** Do not instantly purge your mutation meter after taking minor contamination hits. Save your Stabilizer Syringes for situations where weapon sway makes precision shooting impossible.
- **Use Environmental Hazards:** Lure aggressive stalkers into unstable spatial tears. The crushing gravitational force can eliminate high-tier threats without firing a single bullet.
- **Keep an Empty Rig Slot for Key Artifacts:** Certain anomaly cores decay if left exposed to ambient air. Ensure you have clear inventory space before initiating containment puzzles.
