## Story Overview and Setting: Surviving the Anomaly Zones

Set in near-future Japan during the year 20██, reality itself has begun unraveling due to the sudden emergence of localized reality-warping phenomena known as "Anomaly Zones." These surreal spaces distort gravity, bend physical geometry, and spawn terrifying entities termed simply as "Anomalies."

The narrative centers on **Reona Minazuki**, a 17-year-old runaway surviving on the streets who forms a deep bond with student **Arisa Kato**. When a sudden anomalization event strikes a shopping mall, Arisa goes missing, leaving Reona desperate to uncover the truth. To rescue her companion, Reona signs up with the government-backed **Anomaly Zone Countermeasures Office** as a "Diver"—expendable operatives tasked with plunging into hostile spatial rifts.

## Core Gameplay Systems: Dives, Customization, and Artifacts

The gameplay loop in *Anomalith* alternates between dangerous excursions inside Anomaly Zones and strategic prep work at the home base.

### 1. Spatial Investigation & Tactical Combat
Each Anomaly Zone presents bizarre environmental hazards, such as endlessly replicating shopping alleys and corridors that writhe under shifting physics. Players must engage hostile Anomalies while constantly monitoring remaining ammunition and inventory slots before securing a safe extraction point.

### 2. The 12 Anomalization Powers
Beyond standard ballistic gear, Reona utilizes **12 unique Anomalization Skills**. These powers allow players to manipulate spatial fields, deploy kinetic shockwaves, and counter specific elite Anomalies encountered throughout deep dives.

### 3. Deep Gear Customization
Weapon performance is heavily modifiable. Players can adjust barrels, sights, stocks, and magazine capacities to fit personal playstyles. Furthermore, equipping **Artifacts** extracted from hazardous zones provides direct stat boosts and passive combat enhancements.

| Gameplay Element | Description | Player Strategic Impact |
