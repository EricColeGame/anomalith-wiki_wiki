## Core Story and Setting: The Otherworlds of 19** Japan

Set during a retro-futuristic alternate era labeled as 19**, Japan has become fractured by catastrophic spatial tears called **Otherworlds**. These dimensional anomalies distort physical laws and spawn predatory, unpredictable entities known simply as anomalies.

Players step into the shoes of **Reona Minazuki**, an investigator for the government’s specialized *Danger Zone Countermeasures Office*. Ten years prior, Reona was trapped inside a mysterious anomaly termed the "Sarcophagus." Awakening in a world overrun by terror, she discovers she possesses an innate metaphysical sensitivity to Otherworld fluctuations. Driven by a mission to protect surviving civilians and track down a cherished friend lost in the chaos, Reona plunges straight into the epicenter of these spatial anomalies.

## Combat Mechanics and the Demi-Humanization System

The showcase within the **Anomalith gameplay trailer** confirms that combat is far more dynamic than traditional slow-paced survival horror. Players must alternate between precision third-person shooting, brutal close-quarters combat, and biomechanical supernatural abilities.

| Combat Layer | Primary Function | Tactical Application |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Third-Person Gunplay** | Ranged suppression & weak-point targeting | Neutralizing agile, erratic monsters from safety |
| **Stun Melee Execution** | High-impact finishing blows | Conserving ammunition and staggering heavy foes |
| **Demi-Humanization** | Active & passive anomaly-infused skills | Reality-altering offense, evasions, and crowd control |
| **Artifact Infusion** | Weapon property modification | Adding elemental, debuff, or risk/reward damage modifiers |

### Surviving Erratic Enemy AI

Monsters in *ANOMALITH* do not follow rigid patrolling paths. As evidenced by combat sequences in the **Anomalith gameplay trailer**, anomalies leap off walls, flank through warped geometry, and react violently to auditory cues.

1.  **Staggering Opponents:** Target specific appendages using precision firearms to trigger a vulnerable stun state.
2.  **Executing Melee Submissions:** Close the distance rapidly to execute physical takedowns, saving precious bullet counts.
3.  **Harnessing Demi-Human Abilities:** Absorb defeated anomaly cores to level up Reona's Demi-Humanization skill tree, unlocking telekinetic dashes and defensive barriers.

## Customization, Arsenal, and Lore Collection

Character personalization and tactical preparation form a massive pillar of the core gameplay loop. Between harrowing incursions, Reona returns to the safety of the Countermeasures Base to interact with support staff and customize her gear.

Unlike survival horror games with rigid cosmetic options, *ANOMALITH* features extensive aesthetic and statistical gear slots:

*   **300+ Cosmetic & Gear Pieces:** Modify Reona's appearance across nine unique body regions (headwear, jackets, tactical rigs, gloves, boots, and accessories).
*   **Luck-Based Artifacts:** Equip mysterious relic items that can either grant immense buffs or dangerous trade-offs during incursion runs.
*   **Weapon Modding:** Attach optics, extended magazines, and recoil stabilizers to firearms to match distinct playstyles.

| Item Category | Count / Variety | Gameplay Impact |
